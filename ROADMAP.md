# Faceless Psychology / Self-Help Content — Roadmap & Tool Guide

Goal: Build a faceless Instagram + TikTok brand around psychology and self-help
content, produced with AI tools (free wherever possible), and grow it to the
point where it generates revenue through platform payouts, affiliates, and
digital products.

---

## 1. The Big Picture

The pipeline for every video looks like this:

```
Idea → Script (AI) → Voiceover (AI) → Visuals (cartoon/animated/stock) → Captions → Post → Analyze → Repeat
```

You are building a repeatable content machine. The tools matter less than
consistency: accounts that post 1x/day for 90 days almost always beat accounts
that post perfect videos 2x/week.

### Pick a sub-niche (do this first)

"Psychology" is too broad. Pick ONE angle so the algorithm learns who to show
you to:

| Sub-niche | Example hook |
|---|---|
| Psychology facts / human behavior | "Your brain does this when someone lies to you..." |
| Dark psychology & manipulation awareness | "3 signs someone is gaslighting you" |
| Stoicism / discipline / self-improvement | "Marcus Aurelius said this about overthinking" |
| Habits & productivity psychology | "The 2-minute rule that rewires your brain" |
| Relationship / attachment psychology | "Why anxious and avoidant people attract each other" |

**Important:** Never present content as medical or therapeutic advice. Frame it
as educational/entertainment ("psychology says...", "studies suggest..."). Put
"for educational purposes" in your bio. This also keeps you safer with platform
moderation.

## 2. Tool Options by Pipeline Step

### Step 1 — Scripts (all free)

| Tool | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Claude** (claude.ai) | Free tier | Best for nuanced, well-structured scripts. Give it a template: Hook (first 2 sec) → 3 points → CTA. |
| **ChatGPT** | Free tier | Good for batch-generating 10 script ideas at once. |
| **Gemini** | Free | Solid alternative, generous free limits. |

Prompt template that works well:
> "Write a 60–90 second TikTok script about [topic] in the [sub-niche] niche.
> Start with a scroll-stopping hook under 8 words. Use short, punchy sentences.
> 3 key points, each with a concrete example. End with a question that drives
> comments. Conversational tone, 8th-grade reading level."

### Step 2 — AI Voiceover

| Tool | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok / CapCut built-in TTS** | Free | Zero-friction, and TikTok's algorithm is friendly to its native voices. Weakest quality. |
| **ElevenLabs** | Free tier (~10 min/month), paid from ~$5/mo | The best-sounding AI voices by far. Free tier requires attribution. Worth paying for once you have traction. |
| **Microsoft Clipchamp / Edge TTS** | Free | Surprisingly good neural voices, unlimited, no watermark. |

### Step 3 — Video Creation (the main choice)

**Option A — Manual assembly (free, more control, ~30–45 min/video):**

| Tool | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **CapCut** | Free | The workhorse. Auto-captions, templates, effects, stock media, TTS — all free. Made by ByteDance, exports work great for TikTok AND Reels (export without the TikTok watermark for Instagram). |
| **Canva** | Free tier | Best for animated/cartoonish styles: characters, motion graphics, stock video, voiceover support. Very beginner-friendly. |
| **Kapwing** | Free tier (watermark; 4-min limit) | Browser-based faceless video maker with royalty-free footage, music, and AI voiceover. |

**Option B — AI "one-prompt" faceless video generators (fast, mostly paid):**

| Tool | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **InVideo AI** | Free tier with watermark; ~$20/mo | Type a prompt, get a full video with script, stock media, voiceover, music. Watermark on free makes it demo-only. |
| **StoryShort / AutoShorts / Faceless.video** | ~$19–39/mo | "Autopilot" faceless channels — generate & even auto-post daily. Convenient but output looks generic; algorithms increasingly deprioritize obviously mass-produced AI content. |
| **Pictory** | Paid (~$19/mo+) | Script-to-video with stock footage. Good for list-style content. |

**Option C — Cartoon/animated style specifically (your stated preference):**

| Tool | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Canva (animated elements + characters)** | Free | Build a recognizable "character" or visual identity from free animated assets. |
| **AI image gen (Bing Image Creator, Ideogram free tier)** | Free | Generate a consistent cartoon character/scene set, then animate with pans/zooms in CapCut. This is how most "cartoon psychology" accounts work. |
| **Animaker / Vyond** | Free tier (watermark) / paid | Full character animation. Overkill early on; revisit later. |

### Step 4 — Captions & Polish

- **CapCut auto-captions** — free, accurate, and styled captions are
  non-negotiable (most viewers watch muted).
- Keep videos 9:16, hook in the first 1–2 seconds, text on screen at all times.

### Recommended free starting stack

> **Claude (script) → Clipchamp or ElevenLabs free (voice) → AI images for a
> consistent cartoon character → CapCut (assembly + captions) → post.**
> Total cost: $0. Upgrade ElevenLabs (~$5/mo) first when revenue starts.

## 3. The Phased Roadmap

### Phase 0 — Setup (Week 1)
- [ ] Pick sub-niche and a brand name (short, memorable, niche-relevant).
- [ ] Create TikTok + Instagram (business/creator accounts), same handle, same
      profile art. Bio: what you post + "educational purposes" note.
- [ ] Design your visual identity: 1 caption style, 1 color palette, 1 cartoon
      character or consistent visual motif. Consistency = brand recognition.
- [ ] Study 5 successful accounts in your niche. Save 20 of their best hooks.
- [ ] Batch-produce your first 7 videos before posting anything.

### Phase 1 — Consistency Engine (Weeks 2–8)
- [ ] Post 1x/day on TikTok, cross-post to Reels (remove watermarks!).
- [ ] Batch on weekends: scripts for the week in one sitting, record/assemble
      in another.
- [ ] Mix video lengths: mostly 30–60s, but 2–3 per week **over 60 seconds**
      (this matters for monetization later — see below).
- [ ] Reply to every comment in the first hour (comments are the strongest
      early ranking signal).
- [ ] Weekly review: which 2 videos performed best? Make 2 more like each.

### Phase 2 — Growth (Months 2–4)
- Target: **10,000 followers + 100,000 views/30 days** (the monetization gates).
- Double down on proven formats; kill formats that flop twice.
- Use trending audio (low volume under your voiceover) on Reels.
- Start a content series ("Psychology Fact #37") — series drive follows.
- Add a link-in-bio tool (Beacons/Linktree free) even before you have anything
  to sell — collect the habit and the click data.

### Phase 3 — Monetization (Month 3+, as gates unlock)

**Platform payouts (2026 requirements):**

| Program | Requirements | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok Creator Rewards | 10k followers, 100k views in 30 days, account 30+ days old, 18+, no strikes. **Only original videos longer than 1 minute qualify.** | ~$0.40–$1.00+ per 1k qualified views |
| Instagram (Reels bonuses/gifts, Subscriptions) | Varies; Subscriptions ~10k followers, professional account, 18+, eligible country | Variable |

**The bigger money (usually beats platform payouts):**
1. **Affiliate links** — psychology books (Amazon Associates), apps (BetterHelp
   affiliates, Headspace, etc.), courses. Works from day one, no follower gate.
2. **Digital products** — a $7–15 PDF ("30-day discipline system", journaling
   prompts, habit tracker) sold via Gumroad/Stan Store from your link-in-bio.
   This is where faceless self-help accounts make most of their money.
3. **Brand deals** — inbound once you pass ~20–50k followers in this niche.
4. **YouTube Shorts** — same videos, third platform, additional RPM. Add it
   once the TikTok/IG routine is effortless.

## 4. Critical Warnings (read these)

1. **"Unoriginal content" flags.** Both platforms increasingly demote
   low-effort mass-produced AI slop. Fully-automated one-prompt videos are the
   most at risk, and TikTok's Creator Rewards requires *original* content. Your
   edge: custom cartoon visuals, your own script voice/style, real editing.
   Use AI as the engine, not the whole car.
2. **Label AI content where required.** TikTok requires AI-generated content
   to be labeled in some cases; the toggle costs you nothing — use it.
3. **Don't buy followers, ever.** Kills your reach permanently.
4. **Watermarks kill cross-posts.** Never post a TikTok-watermarked video to
   Reels (Instagram demotes it). Export clean from CapCut for each platform.
5. **Copyright.** Use only stock/royalty-free media from within your tools.
   Don't rip other creators' clips.
